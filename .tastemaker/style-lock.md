## Color contract

Full matrix: `contrast-matrix.txt`, generated with Tastemaker's `check_contrast.py` over every token.

- Text/bg: 16.32:1.
- White label/primary: 5.22:1.
- Text, muted, primary-ink and accent-ink clear 4.5:1 on bg, surface, secondary and paper.
- White labels clear 4.5:1 on primary and primary-hover.
- Hero rose accent is text-safe on bg and paper only. Use accent-ink for small rose text on tinted panels.
- Primary is suitable for large lettering and graphical marks. Use primary-ink for small purple text on tints.
- Input/state borders clear 3:1 on the four light backgrounds. Decorative borders are not state indicators.
- Green is reserved for the availability dot, which clears the 3:1 graphical floor.

The preview's muted #6C666D was darkened within the same neutral family to #625B64 so text on secondary panels also clears AA. Deeper plum/rose ink variants provide readable small labels while retaining the selected primary and accent unchanged.
